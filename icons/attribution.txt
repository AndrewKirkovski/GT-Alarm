## TODO — pre-Play-Store legal hardening

Flaticon's free license requires per-pack attribution. The 16 wired drawables
in android-app/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ (snooze, repeat, tag,
music-note, vibration, info, warning, settings, timer, delete, image, help,
watch, alarm-off, watch-off, delete-forever) likely span multiple Flaticon
authors/packs — not all are guaranteed to be in Pixel-perfect's smartwatch
pack. Before public release, EITHER:
  (a) confirm every drawable is sourced from one Pixel-perfect pack and
      expand this credit per-pack, OR
  (b) re-source all icons from a single same-license pack and document.

This is tracked as a pre-release legal/correctness item in
docs/acceptance-criteria.md → "KNOWN GAPS TO CLOSE NEXT → Android" (entry 0).
