## C. Device-coverage wording (the "donate devices" question — answered)

**Do NOT** put "tested on only one model" or a hardware-donation request in the
store listing. Rejection risk on both stores (Google minimum-functionality +
metadata-must-match-functionality; Huawei §3.4 "incomplete functions" / §1.6).
Hardware-donation asks aren't explicitly banned but are off-purpose listing
content and reinforce an "unfinished" signal.

- **In the listing:** the positive line already used above —
  *"Made for the Huawei Watch GT 6 series."*
- **The contribution ask goes OFF-store** (your website / GitHub / privacy page),
  e.g.: *"Want to help us support more watch models? Get in touch via
  andrew.kirkovski@gmail.com."*

## D. Privacy policy

**Required on both stores — yes, even with no accounts / no data collection.**
- **Google Play:** a privacy-policy URL is mandatory to complete the **Data
  safety** form; required even for zero-data apps. Also fill the Data-safety
  questionnaire (declare the Bluetooth/Wear Engine pairing + any image transfer).
- **Huawei AppGallery (§7):** needs the privacy policy **and an in-app link** to
  it, plus **Privacy Tags / Privacy Label** in AppGallery Connect (Wear Engine is
  a Huawei SDK, so this is triggered).

**Where to host (free, stable, HTTPS):**
- **GitHub Pages** (recommended for you) — free, versioned, stable HTTPS. e.g.
  `https://<user>.github.io/gtwake/privacy` or a `gh-pages` branch.
- **Google Sites** — free, no build step, good if you want zero tooling.
- Draft the text with an open generator (e.g. nisrulz/app-privacy-policy-generator)
  then self-host. Avoid pastebins / Drive links (reviewers fetch the URL; it must
  not 404).

**One URL can serve both apps + both stores** — but the page must **name both
apps** ("GT Wake Companion" and "GT Wake") and describe the data behavior
(Bluetooth/Wear Engine pairing, background image transfer to the watch, no
accounts, no analytics). Also add an in-app "Privacy policy" link (the Help
screen is the natural place) so Huawei §7.1's in-app-link requirement is met.

## E. Screenshots

### Watch (AppGallery — its own watch-specific slot)
- Spec (confirm exact numbers live in AppGallery Connect at upload — these come
  from third-party aggregators, Huawei's spec page wouldn't render):
  **~4–8 screenshots, 840 × 840 px square, PNG/JPG ≤ 2 MB each.** Must match the
  real UI (§1.6) — no fabricated screens.
- **Capture workflow (Lite Wearable, no adb to the watch):**
  1. **Primary — native GT 6 screenshot of the sideloaded app.** Build+push with
     `scripts/build-watch.sh debug`, sideload via App Debug Assistant, open each
     screen on the watch, take the watch's built-in screenshot (swipe-down
     shortcut panel, or the two-button combo — exact gesture varies by firmware;
     confirm on your unit). It saves to the watch and syncs to the phone's
     Gallery / Huawei Health. This is the most review-safe (literal on-device
     render). Source content is 466×466 round.
  2. **Fallback — DevEco Previewer render** of each HML/JS page, screenshot the
     previewer. Pixel-clean and repeatable; still depicts the real app code.
  3. Composite each 466×466 round capture centered on an **840×840** square
     canvas, export PNG ≤ 2 MB, produce 4–8.
- Verify on your physical watch that the screenshot gesture works while a
  sideloaded third-party app is foreground (some Huawei watches restrict it).

- **2–8 screenshots per device type; 320–3840 px per side; longer side ≤ 2×
  shorter; JPEG or 24-bit PNG with NO alpha (transparent PNG is rejected);

### Phone — AppGallery (Android-native app)
